=== TITLE ===
Southern Ring Nebula (NGC 3132) — Hubble WFPC2, 1995

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Planetary nebula NGC 3132 as seen by the Hubble Space Telescope's WFPC2 camera on 1995-12-07. Program 6221, PI John Trauger. The same data underlie the 1998 Hubble Heritage image (STScI-1998-39); this is my own processing from the archive files.

Three narrow-band filters: F502N [O III] blue · F656N Hα green · F658N [N II] red. Blue traces the hottest, doubly ionised oxygen in the interior; red the cooler nitrogen at the rim. Cosmic-ray hits were removed by cross-checking filters; the chip seams of WFPC2 are interpolated (≤ 6 px), the corners outside the field are filled at sky level.

Fully reproducible from a YAML config: https://github.com/kriden187-cloud/skyforge (tag phase-3b, configs/ngc3132hst/showcase.yaml). Companion to my JWST image of the same nebula.
